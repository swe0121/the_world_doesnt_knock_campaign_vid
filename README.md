# The World Doesn't Knock

We made this campaign as a website that plays like a video because we wanted the final output to feel more creative and intense while still following the video requirement. Once it opens, the scenes move automatically with text, photo cards, flashes, transitions, and animations.

## Why we chose this design

We used a dark analog horror style because the campaign talks about serious issues like conflict, disasters, health problems, climate change, economic pressure, and cooperation between countries. The black background, red flashes, scan lines, grain, distorted movement, fast cuts, and photo cards are meant to make the campaign feel tense and urgent.

The fast pacing also represents how quickly something happening in another country can affect the Philippines. We used short statements, quotes, and analogies so the audience can understand one idea quickly before the next scene appears.

## Why the title is The World Doesn't Knock

We chose this title because global events do not ask permission before they affect another country. A pandemic, war, economic problem, disaster, or climate issue can begin somewhere else and still affect the Philippines. The same is true for support, trade, knowledge, technology, and opportunities.

## Main message

Our main message is that independence does not mean isolation. The Philippines can protect its own identity and interests while still cooperating with other countries when problems are too large for one country to handle alone.

## Personal reflections

The campaign includes six separate personal reflections labeled Member 1 to Member 6. These labels can be replaced later with the actual group members' names.

## Controls

Press **Spacebar** to pause or play.

Press **R** to restart.

You can also drag the progress bar at the bottom to move to any part of the campaign.
