# The World Doesn't Knock

We chose to make the campaign as a website that plays like a video because we wanted the presentation to feel more creative and different while still following the video requirement. Once it is opened, the scenes, text, photo cards, flashes, and animations play automatically, so it works more like an edited campaign video than a normal website.

We used a dark and intense design because the topic includes serious issues like disasters, health problems, climate change, economic problems, conflict, and cooperation between countries. The dark colors, red flashes, grain effects, quick cuts, and photo cards are meant to make the message feel more serious and keep the audience focused.

We also used short texts, quotes, and analogies because the pacing is fast. Instead of putting long paragraphs on screen, we wanted each scene to give one clear idea that the audience can understand quickly.

The title **The World Doesn't Knock** means that global events do not ask permission before their effects reach another country. A problem can begin somewhere else and still affect the Philippines through health, trade, prices, disasters, climate, or security. At the same time, help, opportunities, knowledge, and cooperation can also cross borders.

Our main message is that independence does not mean isolation. The Philippines can protect its own interests while still cooperating with other countries when problems are too big for one country to solve alone.

**Controls:** Press **Spacebar** to pause or play. Press **R** to restart.
